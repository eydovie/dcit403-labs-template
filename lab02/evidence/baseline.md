--- How many times has the Ghanian cedi currency been changed? ---
**Answer:**  
The Ghanaian cedi has been redenominated **two times** (i.e., the currency has been “changed” twice).

| Date | Event | What Changed | Conversion Rate |
|------|-------|--------------|-----------------|
| **1965** | Introduction of the cedi | The cedi replaced the Ghanaian pound | 1 cedi = 4.75 pounds (for reference) |
| **1979** | First redenomination (new cedi) | The “old cedi” was replaced by a new cedi | 1,000 old cedis = 1 new cedi |
| **2007** | Second redenomination (new cedi) | The 1979‑issued new cedi was again replaced by a new cedi | 10,000 old cedis = 1 new cedi |
